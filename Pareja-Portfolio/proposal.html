My Portfolio Proposal

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to create a professional portfolio that showcases my skills, projects, education, and career interest in cybersecurity and software engineering. It will give employers a clearer understanding of what I bring to the table by presenting them with my technical abilities and displaying projects I used those abilities in. According to GitHub Docs, a well organized GitHub profile could aid me in showing employers who I am, my best projects and my skills. Similarly, Harvard's John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences explains that an online portfolio will help me stand out from the crowd, it would give employers more to look at and naturally more time on me. This information has influenced my decision to split my website into home,projects, skills, resume, and contact information, so that it’s easier to understand. Ultimately, at the current moment the goal of this project is to break into the cybersecurity field and continue to update it so that in the future I could have better career opportunities.

Target Audience

The target audience for this portfolio are recruiters, hiring managers, and tech professionals looking for students or entry level candidates interested in software development and cybersecurity. This would help them see my skills, education, projects and qualifications.

Components

The website will include 5 components:

  1. The home page: A little introduction about myself, my background, and my career interests.

  2. The Projects page: Will showcase previous and current coding projects I've done and explain how I did them.

  3. The skills page: Lists my current programming languages, cybersecurity knowledge, and other relevant skills.

  4. The Resume page: Displays my resume and previous work experience.

  5. The Contact page: Provides my email, phone number, and social media links.
